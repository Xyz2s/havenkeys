How the job runs

Roofers don't work in steps. We work in courses.

A "course" is one row of shingles, laid so it overlaps the row below it. That's also how we run a project — each stage covers and protects the one before it.

  1. Course 1

    Walkthrough & measure

    A crew lead climbs up, not just looks up — checking deck, slope, ventilation, and every penetration before we quote a single shingle.

  2. Course 2

    Tear-off & deck check

    Old material comes off down to the sheathing. Any soft or rotted decking gets replaced before anything new goes back on — always itemized, never assumed.

  3. Course 3

    Underlayment & flashing

    Ice-and-water shield at eaves and valleys, synthetic felt across the field, new step and counter flashing at every wall and chimney.

  4. Course 4

    Material install

    Shingles, panels, or tile go on in staggered courses to code and manufacturer spec — usually one to three days, weather depending.

  5. Course 5

    Final walk & warranty

    We walk the roof and the yard with a magnetic nail sweep, then hand over your workmanship warranty and manufacturer registration.
